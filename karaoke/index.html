      Woofie's help desk

      How does the queue work?
      Songs play in the order people sign up. You'll see your place on the last screen.
      What does shuffle do?
      The wallpaper changes on every bar of the music. Pick all wallpapers, or just one collection.
      Can I sing the same song twice?
      Sure. Tap "Add another song" after you sign up.
      My song didn't play?
      Some videos can't be played on other sites. Pick a different upload and sign up again.
      Where do I get the code?
      It's on the big screen, next to the QR code.
