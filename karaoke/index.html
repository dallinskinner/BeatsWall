      Woofie's help desk

      How does the queue work?
      Everyone gets a first turn before anyone gets a second. After that it's first come, first served, and the host can move songs around.
      Can I change my song?
      Yes, until it starts. Use "Change song" to keep your spot, or "Cancel".
      How do I know when I'm up?
      Keep this page open. It flashes when you're next and again when it's your turn.
      How do reactions and ratings work?
      Tap an emoji during a song and it floats across the TV. After each song you get a few seconds to give it stars.
      What does shuffle do?
      The wallpaper changes on every bar of the music. Pick all wallpapers, or just one collection.
      Can I sing the same song twice?
      Sure. Tap "Add another song" after you sign up.
      My song didn't play?
      Some videos can't be played on other sites. Pick a different upload and sign up again.
      Where do I get the code?
      It's on the big screen, next to the QR code.
